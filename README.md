# 🌅 Morning Aura – Daily Matrix Planner

**Morning Aura** is a daily productivity and motivation app that combines **task prioritization, streak tracking, and daily fortune inspiration** to help you stay focused, motivated, and consistent.  

Gamify your productivity with **plant growth animations** and maintain streaks while completing daily tasks.

---

## 📝 Features

### 1. Daily Task Matrix
- Prioritize tasks using the **Eisenhower Matrix**:
  - **Do First** – Urgent & Vital tasks  
  - **Schedule** – Important but not urgent tasks  
  - **Delegate** – Urgent but not important tasks  
  - **Eliminate** – Neither urgent nor important tasks  
- Add tasks with **time estimates** and priority tags  
- Visual queue and task management

### 2. Plant Growth & Streak Motivation
- **Grow a plant** as you complete tasks  
- Maintain daily streaks to **stay motivated**  
- Missing a day resets the streak to encourage consistency

### 3. Daily Fortune & Inspiration
- Receive a **positive daily fortune message**  
- Motivational quotes appear **after completing tasks**

### 4. Clean UI with Animations
- Minimalistic, intuitive, and engaging  
- Smooth **animations for plant growth and task completion**

---

## 🚀 How It Works
1. Open the app in the morning.  
2. **Water your plant** to grow it – maintain your streak and unlock a golden day animation like a flower bloom.  
3. Receive **daily fortunes and motivational quotes**.  
4. **Add or view tasks** in the matrix and complete them.  
5. Exit or log out – progress is saved, but missing a day resets the streak.
